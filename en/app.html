(01) Features

All your training.
One single screen.

Designed for the people I train, at home and at the gym: simple, fast, distraction-free.

Daily program

Your session ready when you open the app: exercises, loads, rest times and cues.

WOD timer

For time, EMOM, AMRAP, intervals: the timer that tracks your stations and rounds.

Session tracking

The history of every completed session, with how you felt and my feedback.

Progress

Your times, your loads, your consistency: see how far you've come.

Booking

Pick your next slot at home or at the gym, in two taps.

HYROX mode

The 8 stations, the run between each one and a strategy adapted to your level.
