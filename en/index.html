    (08) Questions

    Before you
    get started.

    How does booking work?

    Choose your session, place and time slot in the form above. WhatsApp opens with your request pre-filled: send it and I'll confirm the appointment.

    Which areas do you cover?

    Casablanca and the surrounding region. Enter your city and neighbourhood when booking: I'll confirm the visit along with the time slot.

    I'm a complete beginner. Is this for me?

    Yes. Every session starts from your actual level. The discovery & assessment session is ideal to start with confidence.

    Do I need equipment at home?

    Not necessarily. The session is built around your space and what you have. We'll discuss it when you book.

    What are your prices?

    Prices are available on request: they depend on the format (home, gym, duo, corporate), location and frequency. Message me on WhatsApp or book directly.

    Do you also work with companies?

    Yes. I coach teams at the company's premises or at the gym: group sessions, challenges and team building. Choose “Corporate package” when booking, enter the number of participants and I'll reply with a quote.

    What happens to my information?

    The website stores nothing and uses no cookies. Your request goes straight to WhatsApp and is only used to organise your session. All the details are in the privacy policy.
