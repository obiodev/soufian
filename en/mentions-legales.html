Recipients

Only Soufiane Zalik receives your messages. They go through WhatsApp (Meta group), which you use under its own terms and its privacy policy. As WhatsApp may process data outside Morocco, this transfer happens at your initiative.

Retention period

  • Waitlist: until the app launches or until you unsubscribe.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, object to and delete your data (articles 7 to 9 of law 09-08; articles 15 to 21 of the GDPR), as well as the right to withdraw your consent at any time. To exercise them, simply message on WhatsApp at +212 632-740500. Reply within 30 days maximum.

If you disagree, you can contact Morocco's National Commission for the Control of Personal Data Protection (CNDP) or, if you live in the European Union, your country's data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

Cookies

This site uses no cookies : no advertising, no tracking, no profiling. That's why no consent banner is displayed.

Anonymous statistics

To know how many people visit the site and tap WhatsApp or the phone number, the site uses GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics tool hosted in the European Union. It sets no cookies, doesn't keep your IP address and can't identify you: only the page viewed, the referring site, the approximate country, the screen size and clicks on the contact buttons are counted, in aggregate.

WhatsApp messages opened from the site include the tag [SITE], so Soufiane knows you found him through the site. You can delete it before sending.

  • Fonts are hosted on the site itself: no calls to Google Fonts.
  • Photos are served from the site: no Instagram content is embedded.
  • The calendar file (.ics) offered after booking is created directly in your browser.

Links to Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok or WhatsApp take you to those platforms, which apply their own cookie rules.

Security

  • The site is served exclusively over HTTPS (encrypted connection).
  • A Content Security Policy (CSP) blocks any script from outside the site.
  • No personal data is stored on the server.
  • External links open without passing on information about the page you came from.
